# Dataset Source and Label-Mapping Decision

**Project:** AI-Driven Emotion Detection & Personalized Learning Support Platform  
**Review date:** 2026-10-20  
**Decision status:** Public-candidate review complete; no dataset is approved or acquired. The Streamlit app remains in demonstration mode.

## Decision summary

No reviewed public candidate supplies licensed, verified, study-challenge text with ground-truth coverage of all five project labels. The target labels remain **Bored**, **Confident**, **Confused**, **Curious**, and **Frustrated**. Do not claim that the data below cover all five or use general emotion classes as substitutes.

The recommended primary strategy is a purpose-built, consent-based collection of non-identifying study-challenge text with participants explicitly self-reporting each of the five labels. Public corpora may be considered for separately identified, partial-label auxiliary experiments after source-specific rights and method review. They must not be merged into the target evaluation set or treated as equivalent ground truth. If collection and approval are not feasible, keep the model in demonstration mode.

## Target label mapping

Mappings below are category-level candidates, not claims that the source text measures a learner’s true internal state. The only mappings recommended for a **partial auxiliary experiment** are exact/direct concepts with the stated source annotation. Nothing here qualifies a public source as the five-label target dataset.

| Project target | Candidate source labels | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Bored** | DAiSEE `boredom` only, in video/behavioral annotations | **Unmapped for text.** No reviewed text candidate provides an approved direct target label. Do not infer boredom from Neutral, low engagement, short text, or the absence of another label. |
| **Confident** | EmpatheticDialogues `Confident` is an exact situation-level label | **Unmapped to the study-challenge target pending reannotation/approval.** Its open-domain situation prompt is not evidence that a learner is confident about a study challenge. DAiSEE remains unmapped to text. No GoEmotions `anger` or `annoyance` mapping is allowed. |

### Labels that must remain unmapped

- GoEmotions/EduAffect labels such as `joy`, `sadness`, `anger`, `fear`, `annoyance`, `optimism`, `pride`, `approval`, and `neutral` do not stand in for any target category. EduAffect’s use of the GoEmotions taxonomy does not change that.
- EmpatheticDialogues `proud`, `hopeful`, `prepared`, `content`, and other positive labels are not substitutes for Confident. `annoyed` or `angry` are not substitutes for Frustrated.
- MEED `Neutral` and `Delight` are not Bored or Confident. The absence of a source class is not a target negative.
- DAiSEE video categories, including exact-name overlaps, remain unmapped into text-only examples because modality and annotation evidence differ.
- A source’s unselected single label must not be expanded into negative labels for all other project categories. For a genuinely multi-label source, only source-supported assessed labels can be treated as known; unresolved categories stay unknown.

## Recommended training-data strategy

1. **Primary target data:** after required institutional/ethics approval, collect short non-identifying study-challenge descriptions and contemporaneous participant self-report for each target category. Let participants select multiple applicable categories. Record positive, explicit negative, and unknown separately. Keep “none of these,” “not sure/prefer not to label,” and skipped responses distinct. The participant report is a self-report about context, not objective emotional ground truth.
2. **Public auxiliary data:** do not acquire any candidate yet. If later approved, use EduAffect or GoEmotions only for the directly supported Confused/Curious labels, and consider MEED only for Confused/Curious/Frustrated after its owner provides rights and provenance evidence. Keep each corpus separately versioned and source-reported. Use partial-label masks; do not claim auxiliary performance as target-task performance. EmpatheticDialogues should remain excluded from target labels unless a human-approved reannotation protocol demonstrates study-challenge relevance. Exclude DAiSEE from the text classifier.
3. **No five-label source today:** no candidate supports all five target categories in the intended text task. Bored and Confident in particular require approved target-task annotations. If those data cannot be ethically and lawfully obtained, do not train or evaluate a five-label model.
4. **Mixed-emotion objective:** public single-label data cannot establish co-occurrence. Genuine mixed-emotion training requires explicit multi-select target annotations. Do not manufacture co-occurrence or treat a one-hot conversion as multi-label ground truth.

This strategy aligns with the pipeline contract: supply only reviewed `text`, `labels`, optional `known_labels`, and a non-identifying `group_id`; use an explicit reviewed mapping file; keep unsupported labels out; and never set `--assume-all-labels-known` for a partial or single-dominant-label source. The pipeline validates structure and destination labels, not source rights, ethical approval, semantic equivalence, or annotation quality.

## Minimum checks before any model training

- **Rights and provenance:** record exact dataset version/revision, owner, source URL, file-level license/terms, attribution, permitted local processing/training, redistribution/model-weight restrictions, and any platform terms. Resolve third-party content and privacy rights. No download until a reviewer signs off.
- **Schema and privacy:** use only contract-approved columns; confirm language and task match; screen/redact accidental identifiers and sensitive details before retained processing; do not place text or identifiers in audits/logs. Confirm the source’s collection/consent basis and retention obligations.
- **Label definitions and semantics:** approve operational definitions/examples for all five labels and a source-to-target mapping rationale. Preserve source labels and provenance. Separate self-report from annotator judgments. Never silently convert absence, uncertainty, or a dominant label into a negative.
- **Annotation quality:** inspect a documented, privacy-safe sample under approved access; check label instructions, annotator/self-report provenance, ambiguity, missingness, and class imbalance. For human annotation, independently double-label an approved subset, report per-label agreement, and define adjudication before training. No raw examples in audit reports.
- **Coverage and splits:** before training, verify every target has adequate positive and explicit-negative/known support in train, validation, and test, with multiple independent groups where applicable. Confirm that available independent group counts and per-split support are sufficient for meaningful per-label metrics. Also verify co-occurring target labels exist if mixed-emotion behavior is to be evaluated. Predeclare quantitative minimum support after a pilot/design review; do not invent a universal number or proceed with unsupported labels. Freeze a group/source-aware split before comparing models and audit duplicate/near-duplicate leakage.
- **Stop rule:** if any target has no defensible source label, inadequate known/positive coverage, unresolved rights, or no valid independent test support, omit that target from any claimed model evaluation and keep the full five-label app in demonstration mode.

## Privacy and consent for supplementary collection

Before collection, obtain the approvals required by the institution and jurisdiction; use guardian permission and participant assent if participants may be minors. Participation must be voluntary and unrelated to grades, services, or evaluation. Explain the purpose, labels, retention/deletion, access, and future model-training use in advance; provide a withdrawal process and describe practical limits on removing a record after model training. Collect no names, IDs, contact details, grades, health/diagnosis information, or other sensitive identifiers. Give a clear instruction not to include such details, minimize free text, redact accidental identifiers before retention, restrict access, and set a deletion schedule. Store only a random non-identifying group token where needed to keep participant records in one split; do not keep a key linking it to identity. Do not send collected text to Gemini as part of dataset work.

## Decisions still requiring human approval

1. Approve whether a consent-based, five-label study-challenge collection is ethically, legally, and operationally feasible; identify the participant population, language(s), consent process, review authority, data steward, retention, withdrawal, and deletion procedure.
2. Approve operational definitions and the self-report instrument, including multi-select, explicit negatives, “none,” uncertainty, and missing responses.
3. Decide whether to include any **partial auxiliary** source. Before any such use, resolve EduAffect’s dataset/file and underlying social-post terms, GoEmotions’ Reddit text reuse rights, and MEED’s owner permission, language, provenance, collection/consent, and license. Confirm that EmpatheticDialogues’ non-commercial license fits the project and, separately, that a reannotation would be methodologically valid before reconsidering it.
4. Approve the eventual data-quality minimums and whether each label has enough independent-group support for training and held-out evaluation. If the requirements cannot be met, retain demo-only behavior.

**No data or model artifacts were downloaded, no labels were generated, and no training or performance evaluation was run for this decision.** The preprocessing pipeline and existing tests were left unchanged.
